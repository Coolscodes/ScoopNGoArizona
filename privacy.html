Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 21, 2026

Scoop N Go Arizona ("we," "us," or "our") provides dog waste removal services across the East Valley of Arizona. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our website or submitting a form, including a form you complete inside Facebook or Instagram, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

We only collect what we need to give you a quote and provide service. That may include:

  • Your name, phone number, and email address
  • Your service address and zip code
  • Details about your yard, such as the number of dogs and how often you want service
  • Any notes or messages you choose to share with us
  • Basic technical data, such as your device type and how you found us, collected automatically when you visit our website

How we collect it

  • Directly from you when you fill out a quote form on our website or call, text, or email us
  • Through lead forms on Facebook and Instagram. When you tap one of our ads and submit an instant form, Meta shares the contact details you provide with us
  • Automatically through cookies and pixels. Our website uses the Meta Pixel to understand which ads bring people to our site and to measure results. See "Advertising and analytics" below

How we use your information

  • To prepare and send you a quote
  • To contact you by phone, text, or email about your quote, scheduling, and service
  • To provide, schedule, and improve our dog waste removal service
  • To process payments for service you request
  • To measure and improve our advertising

Text messages and calls

When you give us your phone number, you agree that we may call or text you about your quote and service. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out of texts at any time by replying STOP, or ask us to stop calling, and we will honor your request.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with trusted service providers who help us run our business, and only as needed. These include:

  • Stripe to process payments securely. We never see or store your full card number
  • Supabase, which securely stores your quote requests and customer records for us
  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram) for advertising and lead forms, as described below

We may also share information if required by law, or to protect our rights, safety, or property.

Advertising and analytics

We advertise on Facebook and Instagram and use the Meta Pixel on our website. This helps us see which ads lead to quote requests and show our ads to people who are more likely to be interested. Meta may use this data under its own Data Policy. You can adjust your ad preferences in your Facebook and Instagram settings.

How long we keep your information

We keep your information for as long as needed to provide service and run our business, and to meet legal or tax requirements. You can ask us to delete your information at any time, and we will do so unless we are required to keep it.

Your choices

  • Ask us what information we have about you
  • Ask us to correct or delete your information
  • Opt out of marketing texts or emails at any time

To make any of these requests, just contact us using the details below.

Children

Our services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
